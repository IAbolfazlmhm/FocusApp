Help & Guide

FocusApp has three parts: a Pomodoro timer for focused work sessions, a habit tracker, and a progress dashboard. Everything below is a quick reference — tap a section to expand it, or jump straight to one.

Getting Started

The three tabs at the top switch between the app's main sections: Pomodoro (timer + today's tasks), Habits (daily/weekly habit tracking), and Progress (your stats over time).

Everything you create — tasks, habits, tags, quotes — is saved only in this browser, automatically, as you go. There's no account and nothing is sent anywhere.

Whenever you're unsure what something does, tap the ? icon at the top of any section, or open Settings → Help & Guide to come back here.

Pomodoro Timer

Tap Start to begin a work session. The ring fills in as time passes, and the app cycles automatically through Work → Short Break → Long Break (shown in the session tracker, e.g. "Work 1/4").

  • Skip jumps straight to the next phase.
  • Reset resets the current phase; double-click or press and hold it for a full session reset.
  • The glasses icon opens Focus Mode — a distraction-free full timer view.
  • The target icon shows which task is currently locked in as your focus.

In Settings you can switch to Stopwatch mode (count up instead of down), change the work duration, enable/disable breaks, auto-start breaks, and pick the alert sound.

Tasks

Type a task into the input at the bottom of the Pomodoro tab and tap Add. Tap a task to lock it in as your current focus — its name then shows next to the target icon above the timer, and completed focus time is tracked against it.

  • The tag icon next to the input lets you assign a color tag before adding.
  • Use the sliders icon to Manage Tags (create, recolor, rename, delete).
  • The sort icon lets you order tasks by Newest, Time Spent, A-Z, or by Tag.
  • Use the arrows next to the date to review or plan tasks for other days.
Habits

Use Quick add habit at the bottom for a simple daily habit, or open the pencil icon next to it for full control over a habit's name, category, icon, and goal cadence.

  • Goal can be Every Day, a specific weekday, Custom Days (pick any combination), or Every N Weeks/Months.
  • Tap a habit to mark it done for the selected day; your streak builds as long as you keep meeting the goal.
  • The sliders icon opens Manage Categories to group habits and color-code them.
  • The ring and "Top Streaks" panel above the list summarize how the selected day (or your best streaks) are going.
Progress Dashboard

This tab summarizes your Focus Time, Items Completed, and Perfect Days over a date range, with a consistency ring showing your overall success rate.

  • Switch the date range (e.g. This Week) or pick a Custom Range from the date navigator below the stats.
  • Tap any day in the list to open its Daily Report — a full breakdown of that day's tasks and habits.
  • The gear icon opens Dashboard Settings, where you can toggle Dark Mode, sound effects, and which data (Pomodoro/Habits/comparison arrows) the dashboard shows.
Your Data & Backup

Everything is stored only in this browser (localStorage) — there's no cloud sync. Clearing site data, private browsing, or resetting the browser can erase it.

  • Export Data (in Settings) downloads a JSON backup you can keep or move to another browser/device.
  • Import Data restores from a backup file — this replaces what's currently saved, so use it carefully.
  • Trash holds deleted tasks, habits, tags, categories, and quotes so you can restore them, until you empty it.
Tips & Shortcuts
  • Space starts or pauses the timer (when you're not typing in a field).
  • Esc closes the topmost open dialog, or exits Focus Mode if nothing is open.
  • Press and hold the Reset button for a full session reset instead of just the current phase.
  • Dark Mode and Sound Effects can be toggled from either Settings or the Progress dashboard's own settings.
